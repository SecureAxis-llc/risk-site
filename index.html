The tools are there. The proof isn't.

Mid-sized companies under PCI DSS, GDPR or cyber-insurance scrutiny usually own the security tools already. What they lack is the evidence layer, and the people, to turn those tools into proof.

01

Reporting you can't prove

Monthly risk numbers are stitched together by hand from six to eight consoles. When the board or an auditor asks where a number came from, nobody can show the file.

02

Risk as opinion, not money

Most organisations still can't express cyber risk in money, so budgets and insurance answers rest on judgement.

03

One question, four frameworks

NIST, CIS, PCI DSS and GDPR each ask for the same evidence in different shapes, and it gets gathered four times.

How it works

  1. Bring in what you already runVulnerability scanners, EDR, identity, asset inventory, MDR reports and awareness training, by API or file export. For example Qualys, SentinelOne and Microsoft Entra ID.
  2. Keep the evidenceEvery source file is hashed, kept under a stated retention policy, and every access to it is logged.
  3. Map onceOne crosswalk answers NIST CSF 2.0, CIS Controls v8, PCI DSS 4.0.1 and GDPR, so evidence is gathered once.
  4. Report and respondA monthly report in the format your leadership already reads, plus approved response actions through your own EDR with SecureAxis Response.

Rules it doesn't break

  • Empty, never guessed. A value the parser can't read stays empty and fillable. Never last month's value, never an estimate presented as fact.
  • Works with what you own. It reads from your existing tools instead of replacing them.
  • Runs in your environment. Self-hosted, so your evidence never has to leave your network.
  • Percentages carry their totals. “40% covered” always says “of how many devices”.

What the monthly report covers

Vulnerabilities

Severity trends, fix-deadline compliance, quick wins and devices that keep coming back.

Tool coverage

Which devices lack EDR, where tools disagree about what exists, and coverage by business unit.

Incident response

Detection, containment and response times taken from your MDR's monthly report, with the escalation funnel.

Security awareness

Phishing-test click rates, repeat clickers, and whether assigned training was completed.

Frameworks

NIST CSF 2.0 with maturity scoring, CIS Controls v8, PCI DSS 4.0.1 with SAQ profiles, and GDPR.

Evidence vault

A SHA-256 hash per file, 24-month retention, legal hold and a full access log. Files holding bulk employee data are kept as hashes only.

Questions

Does it replace our security tools?

No. It reads from the tools you already run, by API or file export, and turns their output into traceable reporting.

Where does our data live?

In your environment. SecureAxis Risk is self-hosted, so evidence files and reports stay on your network.

Which frameworks does it map to?

NIST CSF 2.0 with maturity scoring, CIS Controls v8, PCI DSS 4.0.1 with SAQ profiles, and GDPR. It names the controls and links your evidence to them; it doesn't reproduce the standards' text.

What happens when a number can't be read?

It stays empty and is flagged for someone to fill in. The report never carries last month's value forward or presents an estimate as a fact.

How long is evidence kept?

24 months by default, above PCI DSS's 12-month minimum, with legal hold when you need it. Every access to the vault is logged.

Is it ready to buy?

Not yet. It's in development, and pilots are open for a small number of organisations.
